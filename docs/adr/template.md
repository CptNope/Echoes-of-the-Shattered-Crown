## Context

Describe the problem, current constraints and why the choice matters. Link relevant code, roadmap D-IDs and backlog items. Separate facts from assumptions.

## Decision

State one concrete choice or proposed direction. Name what remains unresolved. A Proposed record is not implementation or approval.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Benefits and costs |
| --- | --- |
| Existing approach | Explain whether retaining it is viable |
| Candidate alternative | Explain the relevant tradeoff |

## Consequences

Describe benefits, costs, constraints and operational effects. Address determinism, persistence, accessibility, performance, content production or deployment where relevant.

## Evidence and verification

Link actual evidence and define missing acceptance work. Distinguish current observations from future checks. Do not claim tests or device certification that do not exist.

## Revisit conditions

State which observations or requirements would justify reconsidering this choice.

## Related records
